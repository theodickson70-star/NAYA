Everyone Passengers Drivers Privacy

This is an agreement between you and NAYA. Please read it carefully. By signing up or continuing to use NAYA, you accept these terms. If you do not agree, do not use the service.

Part A: Terms for all users

1. What NAYA is

NAYA is a digital platform (app and website) that connects passengers with independent motorbike (bodaboda) and bajaji drivers. We started in Urambo, Tabora region. NAYA does not own vehicles and does not provide transport itself: the transport is provided by the driver. NAYA's job is to connect you with a nearby driver, show the price, support trip safety and handle complaints.

2. Your account

  • You must be 18 or older to open an account. A parent or guardian who requests a trip for a child is responsible for that child's safety.
  • Give your real name and a phone number that is yours. One person uses one account. The same account can be used as a passenger and as a driver.
  • Protect your password and never give it to anyone, even someone claiming to work for NAYA. The NAYA office will never ask for your password.
  • You are responsible for everything done with your account. If you think someone else has accessed your account, change your password and tell us immediately through Account → Help.

3. Acceptable behaviour

Passengers and drivers must respect each other. The following are forbidden:

  • insults, threats, sexual or any other harassment, and violence;
  • discrimination based on tribe, religion, gender, disability or origin;
  • driving or travelling while drunk or under the influence of drugs;
  • carrying weapons, drugs, smuggled goods or anything illegal;
  • requesting or accepting fake trips, using someone else's account, or trying to cheat or manipulate the system;
  • using another person's phone number obtained through NAYA for anything unrelated to the trip.

4. Road safety

  • On a motorbike, the driver and the passenger must wear a helmet for the whole trip.
  • A motorbike carries only one passenger. A bajaji carries the number of passengers allowed by its licence.
  • Before getting on, check that the name, photo, vehicle type and plate number match those in the app. Do not board a different vehicle.
  • The trip starts only when the passenger gives the driver their 4-digit PIN. The passenger can share their trip with family or a friend through a link.
  • The Emergency button in the app alerts the NAYA office immediately. If you are in real danger, also call 112. Using the Emergency button without reason is an offence.

5. Ratings and feedback

After a trip, the passenger and the driver can give each other stars and comments. Ratings must be honest. The office uses them to monitor service quality and safety. You can also give feedback about NAYA at any time through Account → Give feedback.

6. Account suspension

NAYA may suspend your account temporarily or close it permanently if you break these terms, if there are safety complaints under investigation, if you give false information, or if the law requires it. We will normally tell you the reason. For an urgent safety matter, we may suspend first and inform you afterwards. If you think we made a mistake, contact us through Help and we will review the decision.

7. Limits of NAYA's responsibility

  • The driver is responsible for the trip, their vehicle, their licences and their insurance. NAYA makes efforts to verify drivers, but cannot guarantee everyone's conduct at all times.
  • The app may be unavailable for a while because of the network, the phone or maintenance. We cannot promise that you will always find a driver.
  • To the extent allowed by law, NAYA is not responsible for indirect losses, such as being late for work or losing business.
  • Nothing in these terms removes your rights under the laws of Tanzania, or NAYA's responsibility for death or injury caused by our negligence.

8. Complaints and disputes

If you have a problem, report it through Account → Help. The office will reply in the app and by notification. We will try to settle every dispute by discussion. If that fails, these terms are governed by the laws of the United Republic of Tanzania, and disputes will be decided by the courts of Tanzania.

9. Changes to the terms

We may change these terms as the service grows. If important changes are made, we will tell you in the app and you will be asked to accept them before continuing. The current version is shown at the top of this page.

Part B: Passenger terms

1. Requesting a trip

Choose where you start and where you are going. The app shows you the price before you request. Your request is sent to nearby drivers, and a driver may accept or decline it. When a driver accepts, you will see their name, vehicle, plate and location.

2. Price

  • The price you see when you request is the price of that trip. For routes with a fixed office price (for example town to the bus stand), the price is the one set. For other routes, the price is estimated from the distance and fixed when you request.
  • The driver must not ask for more than the price shown. If they do, pay the app price and tell us through Help.
  • If you ask to go further or to stop on the way, any extra price is an agreement between you and the driver. It is better to request a new trip.

3. Payment

For now you pay the driver in cash when the trip ends. When mobile payments (M-Pesa, Airtel Money, Mixx by Yas, HaloPesa) start, we will announce it. NAYA will never ask you to pay to a private person's number.

4. Waiting and cancelling

  • Be ready at the pickup point you chose. If the driver arrives and cannot find you, they may cancel after waiting a short time.
  • There is currently no cancellation fee. However, cancelling often without reason wastes drivers' time and may lead to your account being restricted.

5. Luggage and forgotten items

  • You may carry small luggage that can be carried safely. The driver may refuse luggage that is large, dangerous or illegal.
  • If you forget something, report it through Help → "I forgot something". The office will help you contact the driver, but NAYA is not responsible for lost items.

6. Damage

If you damage the driver's vehicle on purpose or through carelessness, you are responsible for paying for the damage.

Part C: Driver terms

These terms apply in addition to Part A. You accept them when you submit your application to become a NAYA driver.

1. Your relationship with NAYA

You are an independent service provider, not an employee of NAYA. You decide when to go online, and you may decline any request. NAYA brings you customers through the platform, and you pay a fee to use the platform.

2. Requirements and documents

To be approved, you must be 18 or older and provide:

  • a clear photo of your face;
  • a valid driving licence of the class for your vehicle;
  • your National ID (NIDA);
  • a photo of the vehicle showing the plate number;
  • valid vehicle insurance.

You must also have a licence or permit to carry passengers from LATRA or the relevant authority where the law requires it, and a valid vehicle registration. The office may ask for new documents when they expire or need to be checked again. Providing forged documents leads to immediate account closure, and we may inform the authorities.

3. Your vehicle

  • The vehicle must be safe, serviced, and the same one you registered, with the same plate.
  • On a motorbike, carry two clean helmets: yours and the passenger's.
  • Do not let anyone else drive using your account. The driver who arrives must be the person in the app photo.

4. Monthly fee

  • NAYA does not take a percentage of your fares. The whole fare is yours.
  • Instead, you pay a monthly fee to use the platform. The amount and how to pay are shown in Account → Monthly fee. A new driver may get a free trial period as shown in the app.
  • When the fee expires, you get a few days to pay. After that you will not receive new requests until you pay.
  • If the fee amount changes, we will tell you at least 14 days in advance. A fee already paid is not refunded, except where the office made a mistake recording the payment.

5. Doing trips

  • When you accept a request, go straight to the passenger and update each step in the app: arrived, trip started, trip completed.
  • Do not start a trip without the passenger's correct PIN.
  • Charge only the price shown in the app. Do not ask for more.
  • Follow all road safety rules: safe speed, helmets, one passenger on a motorbike, and no phone in your hand while driving.
  • Accepting requests and then cancelling them often harms the service and may lead to suspension.

6. Your location (GPS)

When you are online, the app sends your location so that it can bring you nearby requests and show the passenger where you are coming from. Go offline when you finish work. When you are offline, your location is not sent.

7. Suspension

Besides the reasons in Part A, the office may suspend you for serious or repeated passenger complaints, a continuing low rating, expired or forged documents, asking for more than the app price, or an unpaid fee. You will see the reason in the app, and you can contact the office through Help.

8. Taxes, licences and fines

You are responsible for your own taxes, licences, permits, insurance and fines related to your transport work.

9. Leaving

You may stop being a NAYA driver at any time: switch your mode to Passenger, or ask through Help for your driver account to be closed.

Part D: Privacy Policy

We protect your information in line with Tanzania's Personal Data Protection Act (Act No. 11 of 2022). This part explains what we collect, why, and your rights.

1. Information we collect

  • Account: name, phone number, password and language. The password is stored in a secret form (hash), so even we cannot read it.
  • Trips: pickup and destination, time, price, ratings, comments, and cancellation reasons.
  • Drivers: vehicle details, documents (licence, NIDA, photos, insurance) and your location while online or on a trip.
  • Help and feedback: the messages you send to the office and its replies.
  • Device: a special number for sending notifications to your phone, plus technical records such as IP address and sign-in time, for security.

2. How we use it

We use it to connect you with a driver or passenger, show prices, verify drivers, protect trip safety (PIN, trip sharing, Emergency), answer complaints, send notifications and service messages, improve NAYA, and meet legal requirements. We do not sell your information to anyone, and we do not use it for other companies' advertising.

3. Who sees it

  • During a trip: the passenger sees the driver's name, photo, vehicle, plate, phone number and location. The driver sees the passenger's name, phone number, and the pickup and drop-off points.
  • The NAYA office: only authorised staff, for service, verification and safety work. Every office action is recorded.
  • Our service providers: cloud servers that store data, a notification service (Google Firebase) and an SMS service (Beem Africa). Some of these servers may be outside Tanzania. We use providers that protect information properly, as the law requires.
  • Authorities: we give information to the Police or courts only when the law requires it, or when someone's life is in danger.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your information only as long as needed to provide the service. If you ask for your account to be deleted, we will delete or anonymise your information within 90 days. Records the law requires us to keep, such as trip and payment records, will remain for the required period.

5. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • know what information we hold about you, and get a copy;
  • correct wrong information;
  • object to certain uses of your information;
  • ask for your information to be deleted;
  • stop receiving offers and promotional messages. Essential service and safety messages will continue.

Send your request through Account → Help. If you feel we have not handled your complaint properly, you can complain to the Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC).

6. Protecting information

All communication goes over a secure connection (HTTPS). Passwords are stored in secret form. Driver documents are visible only to the office. Every office action is recorded. No system is completely without risk, but if we discover a security problem that affects you, we will tell you.

7. Contact us

For any question about these terms or your privacy, use Account → Help in the NAYA app, or visit the NAYA office in Urambo.
